# Annotation Conclusion

## Summary

Functional annotation was successfully completed for all four DNA sequencing samples using the **Ensembl Variant Effect Predictor (VEP)**.

The annotation process linked genomic variants to genes, transcripts, and predicted biological consequences, providing valuable insight into the potential functional effects of the identified variants.

---

## Key Outcomes

- All VCF files were successfully annotated.
- Gene-level and transcript-level information was obtained.
- Functional consequences were assigned to identified variants.
- The annotated dataset is suitable for downstream biological interpretation and genomic analysis.

---

## Pipeline Completion

The successful completion of the VEP annotation marks the final stage of the DNA-Seq Variant Calling and Functional Annotation Pipeline.
